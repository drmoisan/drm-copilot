## Root Cause

This feature delivers BOTH the kickoff-contract consumer (`scripts/dev_tools/parallel_kickoff_contract.py` with its helper `scripts/dev_tools/_parallel_kickoff_tables.py`, and the TypeScript parity port `extensions/drm-copilot/src/lib/validate/parallel-kickoff-artifact.ts`) AND the authoritative producer of documents for that consumer (the fenced `markdown` template under `## Kickoff Artifact` in `.claude/skills/parallel-plan/SKILL.md`). The two were verified independently — the consumer against hand-authored fixtures reaching 100% line and branch coverage, the producer against heading-presence assertions — and never against each other. They disagree in two places, and no test binds them.
