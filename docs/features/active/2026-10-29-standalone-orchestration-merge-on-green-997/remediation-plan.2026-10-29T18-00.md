Design decisions:

- `--admin` is declined in stage C of `Get-EpicMergeGateGreenCiDecision`, silently (empty suffix, no process call), through the shared `Test-CommandLineFlag` parser already used for `--match-head-commit`. A silent decline falls through to the standalone-authorization branch and the final deny, so no existing deny text changes. `--merge`, `--squash`, and `--rebase` behavior is unchanged.
- A command carrying `--admin` with a fully valid green checkpoint therefore ends in the unchanged final deny text, and the live-head seam is not invoked.
- Existing tests that mention `--admin` live in `enforce-epic-merge-gate.MergeQueue.Tests.ps1`, `enforce-epic-merge-gate.Authorization.Tests.ps1`, and the Codex hook suites. They assert merge-queue and authorization behavior that runs before or after the green-CI branch and are unaffected (disposition: unchanged). `enforce-epic-merge-gate.GreenCiRegression.Tests.ps1` and `enforce-epic-merge-gate.GreenCi.Tests.ps1` assert branches 1 to 4 and the pure functions and are unaffected (unchanged); the executor confirms by a test-tree search for `--admin` and `Get-EpicMergeGateGreenCiDecision` in P1-T1.
