## Executive Summary

The branch implements issue #997. A standalone orchestration (neither an epic child nor a parallel item) could not merge its own pull request after CI was green; the merge gate offered only the hand-written #670 authorization record. The branch adds a fail-closed green-CI branch (3b) to `.claude/hooks/enforce-epic-merge-gate.ps1`, evaluated after the parallel allow-branch and before the #670 branch, with all new logic in a dot-sourced sibling `enforce-epic-merge-gate-green-ci.ps1`. Stages A to C are pure; stage D makes one bounded `gh pr view` call through the existing `Invoke-DocsOnlyGhExe` seam; stage E compares the live head with `ci_gate.head_sha` and an optional `--match-head-commit` value. Prose in two rules and two skills, the bundle mirrors, and `core.json` are updated.
