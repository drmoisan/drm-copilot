## Why the `HEAD` operand is required

`[P6-T8]` mandates the `HEAD` operand and the mandate is honoured. A bare `git diff` compares the
worktree against the **index**, so it reports nothing once a change has been staged and would read
as clean whether or not a declared non-write had been violated. `git diff HEAD` compares the worktree
and index against the `HEAD` commit, which closes that particular hole.
