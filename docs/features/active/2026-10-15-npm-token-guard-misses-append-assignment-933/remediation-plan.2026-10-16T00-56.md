## Observed Behavior of the Unchanged Pattern for Every New Row

The pattern at `tests/scripts/dev_tools/npm_token_guard_test_support.py` line 47 is `(?<![\w.-])[\"']?NPM_TOKEN[\"']?\s*:|\bNPM_TOKEN\s*\+?=(?!=)` with `re.IGNORECASE`, applied by `pattern.search` to each line. Alternative A requires a `:` after the name (with optional quote and whitespace) and no word character, `.`, or `-` before it. Alternative B requires a word boundary before the name, then optional whitespace, an optional `+`, an `=`, and no `=` immediately after.

| Row text | Alternative A | Alternative B | Finder result | Row expects | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `env.NPM_TOKEN += x` | no (`.` precedes the name; no `:`) | yes: `\b` between `.` and `N`, `\s*` takes the space, `\+?` takes `+`, `=` matches, next char is a space | `[1]` | `[1]` | pass |
| `MY-NPM_TOKEN+=x` | no (`-` precedes the name; no `:`) | yes: `\b` between `-` and `N`, `\+?` takes `+`, `=` matches, next char is `x` | `[1]` | `[1]` | pass |
| `(( NPM_TOKEN += 1 ))` | no (no `:` after the name) | yes: `\b` between space and `N`, `\s*` takes the space, `\+?` takes `+`, `=` matches, next char is a space | `[1]` | `[1]` | pass |
| `NPM_TOKEN++=x` | no (no `:`) | no: with `\+?` = `+` the `=` meets the second `+`; with `\+?` empty the `=` meets the first `+`; the name occurs once, so no other start position exists | `[]` | `[]` (non-matching test) | pass |
